Uses the same study request and the local body model selected in section 3.

Optional motion in the scenario (ftx-drape-motion/1) continues that same sewn cloth through
declared time/arm-angle keyframes, preserving velocities and material history. It uses the selected shape without refitting.
Each interval follows checked linear body-vertex chords, not a certified anatomical joint path. The report retains every accepted
adaptive substep, maximum panel-edge strain and independent steady endpoint heat.
Expand a successful result to inspect exact retained body/cloth samples from front or side at their recorded physical times.
The sample slider uses one fixed metric scale across the trajectory and both views; it does not interpolate missing states.
Failed motion publishes no partial trajectory; initial static results stay separate. Choose motion_max_absolute_edge_strain_pct to compare the maximum strain across the complete trajectory.
Requiring motion, transient_surface or transient_coverage excludes incomplete histories from the computed frontier.
Optional motion.transient_surface and motion.transient_coverage carry temperatures through every accepted sample.
They require sourced heat capacity plus their own initial temperatures, interval films and budgets. Coverage also requires exact body/layer assignments
and explicit exposed-cloth conditions. The results separately report physical stored heat, signed boundary energy and remap error;
these histories are not interchangeable with each other or steady power snapshots. A heat refusal preserves successful mechanics.
Either thermal history can request plastic_dissipation_heat: "uniform_reference_thickness" and/or
relaxation_dissipation_heat: "uniform_reference_thickness" when its materials supply independent conversion observations
for permanent yielding or Maxwell dashpot work. Accepted work follows the material through covered and exposed regions; each source keeps
its own work ledger and their heat is summed once. Initial forming work, reversible storage, numerical loss and bare body are excluded.
Motion heat objectives use transient_surface_ or transient_coverage_ with inside_energy_j,
net_boundary_energy_j, final_min_temperature_c or final_max_temperature_c.
Use min_sample_temperature_c or max_sample_temperature_c for extrema across the initial state and retained sample endpoints;
these differ from the final endpoint temperature and are not continuous-time bounds.
Select minimize or maximize explicitly: signed energy can reverse with the environment. Internal sources and boundary exchange contribute separately to stored heat.
No two-way thermomechanical, body self-intersection, seated/squat or empirical ride-up claim is made.


Scenario comparisons require a settled initial drape with clear gating. Static heat, strain and simulated-cloth-mass objectives describe
that initial frame. Motion objectives additionally require complete, converged continuation; the final moving state need not be at rest.
Signed surface power and body-coverage power have different bases and are not interchangeable.
There is no hidden weighted score, whole-wearer warmth rating or global garment winner.
