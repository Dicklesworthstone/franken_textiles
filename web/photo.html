Photo measurements

Measure lengths or angles using a flat reference sheet in the photo: blank US Letter/A4 or a printed ChArUco calibration board, with optional existing lens calibration. Photos and clicks stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded or saved in browser storage. Return to the Pattern Workbench.

1. Find the reference sheet

Show all four paper edges against a darker background. Verify the actual paper size and keep the sheet flat, square to the camera and near the landmarks' plane. A detected reference cannot prove its printed size or remove depth error.

Use the JSON sidecar belonging to the printed board. The sheet supplies its physical dimensions and marker IDs. Print at actual size and check its ruler bars. Download a matching Letter board and Letter sidecar, or A4 board and A4 sidecar.

Use a saved ftx-camera-calibration/1 report or ftx-photo-lens/1 document for the exact lens, focus, zoom, resolution and orientation. Retained calibration is checked against its saved observations. These fields assert the current capture settings; they do not rotate or resize the photo. Known calibration-photo reuse is refused when uncertainty requires independence. This page consumes calibration; identify the camera with the native workflow.

Choose a photo. PNG must be opaque, already oriented 8-bit gray/RGB; JPEG EXIF is applied once.

Limits: 64 MiB encoded and 16 million pixels per photo; 256 KiB sheet/point JSON; 16 MiB retained calibration. Unsupported HEIC/WebP, transparency and animation require conversion; images are never silently resized.
