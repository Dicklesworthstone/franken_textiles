Photo measurements

Measure lengths or angles using a flat, blank US Letter or A4 sheet in the photo. Photos and clicks stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded or saved in browser storage. Return to the Pattern Workbench.

1. Find the reference sheet

Show all four paper edges against a darker background. Verify the actual paper size and keep the sheet flat, square to the camera and near the landmarks' plane. A detected rectangle cannot prove its size or remove depth error. ChArUco boards and calibrated lenses use the CLI workflow.

Choose a photo. PNG must be opaque, already oriented 8-bit gray/RGB; JPEG EXIF is applied once.

Limit: 64 MiB encoded and 16 million pixels. Unsupported HEIC/WebP, transparency and animation require conversion; images are never silently resized.
