Pattern Workbench

Draft a sewing pattern from measurements, check every seam and fit point, and download print-at-home PDFs and cutter files. It runs FrankenTextiles in this page (Rust compiled to WebAssembly), so nothing you type leaves your browser. Open the material workbench to inspect source records, change per-piece materials, and recompute drape, heat, cutting, sewing and costs. You can also trace, name and scale a PNG pattern sheet, then load its pattern JSON here for garment and measurement checks.

1. Garment

Without a fit spec the checks use role-based defaults.

2. Body

Measure photo landmarks locally with a blank Letter/A4 reference, then send or add their records below alongside stature and the other required measurements.

Tape measurements in cm (degrees where marked). ● = needed for this garment; the rest improve it. Empty boxes fall back to the size chart for this height, shown greyed, and the check below says which ones still need measuring.

Add photo or other measurement evidence

Choose My tape measurements or a primary Measurements file, then add local fabric-measurements/1 files. Their original records are fused with the primary body's records; definitions, uncertainty, conflicts and MODEL_FIT restrictions still apply.

Up to 8 files, 16 MiB combined and 4096 total records. Files stay local and their exact text is retained in the round's measurements download. The earliest known date is used only when all dates are known; otherwise the combined date is unknown. Review source dates yourself. A clicked length does not establish a girth or physical fit. Size-chart demos cannot be combined with wearer evidence.
Print a photo scale reference

Download a ChArUco sheet for your paper size. Print the PDF at 100% / Actual size, turn off Fit to page, and check the 100 mm and 4 in bars along both axes with a ruler. Tape the sheet flat to stiff card before taking photos.
