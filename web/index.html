Add photo or other measurement evidence

Choose My tape measurements or a primary Measurements file, then add local fabric-measurements/1 files. Their original records are fused with the primary body's records; definitions, uncertainty, conflicts and MODEL_FIT restrictions still apply.

Up to 8 files, 16 MiB combined and 4096 total records. Files stay local and their exact text is retained in the round's measurements download. The earliest known date is used only when all dates are known; otherwise the combined date is unknown. Review source dates yourself. A clicked length does not establish a girth or physical fit. Size-chart demos cannot be combined with wearer evidence.

Use a fitted body (optional)

Load the converted body model to draft, check targets or drape a garment. The model fits the current tape evidence, or reuses an accepted image-fit shape below. All files stay local.

Use anny-0.6.1-default.ftxbody, made with tools/body-model/convert_anny.py, or the exact model used for the accepted fit.

Reuse an accepted image-fit shape

Choose the saved ftx-body-fit/1 report. Its model must match, and its accepted shape is reused without tape refitting. Image-frame poses are not used for drafting or draping.

New patterns use the model's dimensions and placement in its reference pose. Imported and altered patterns keep their geometry and are checked against that body. Tape fitting needs the wearer's age and a supported model. Boleros still need a supplied hand-passage value; trousers need foot passage. Model predictions do not add measured evidence or establish physical fit.

Every option above is opt-in and omitted from settings when left at its legacy default. Air drag, uncalibrated stick-slip friction, and hem/seam multipliers are structural approximations, not measured textile constants or physical-fit claims; characterized material records refuse uncalibrated stick-slip or construction overrides.

      

      
Static equilibrium runs a separate frictionless conservative solve from the finished dynamic state without altering dynamic frames, flags, or simulated time. History-dependent materials refuse the static comparison while retaining the dynamic drape.

      
Projected Newton with Implicit Euler is the default solver. Opt-in Generalized-α (ρ∞ ∈ [0, 1]; 0 strongest high-frequency damping, 1 zero numerical dissipation) refuses history-dependent materials and may require smaller steps on stiff contact transitions. Custom schedule overrides stay within the 512-step browser budget per sewing path (sew + gravity + settling).

      
Retries a stalled step as smaller steps without extending simulated time. The trial limit covers sewing, draping and settling, separately for each start. Exhausting the limits refuses the run.

      
Arm angle applies to tape-fitted or accepted shapes; the default is 35°.
        Leg fraction moves from standing (0) to the full selected posture (1), keeping the fitted shape and arm angle.
        Extra settling continues the sewn garment until its quiet window or the step limit; 0 keeps the fixed schedule.
        History-dependent materials require a fixed-duration run and refuse automatic settling.

      
The optional comparison runs a second full solve at 18 sewing steps versus the
        ordinary 12-step path. It can roughly double fold time. Agreement is sensitivity evidence,
        not a confidence interval, unique-equilibrium proof or physical-fit claim.
